Baking Fresh Every Morning

We fire up the ovens at 4:00 AM every day so you can grab hot bread and fresh pastries on your way to work. Everything we make uses simple, organic flour from local mills.

What We Do Best

People know us best for our long-ferment sourdough loaves, flaky butter croissants, and custom weekend cakes. Whether you're dropping by for a morning espresso or picking up bread for family dinner, we've got something warm waiting for you.

What We Bake Daily

We stock our counter every morning at 6:30 AM. We don't sell items directly through the website, but you can put in a pre-order on our contact page if you want us to hold something for you.

Fresh Breads

Our bread dough sets for 24 hours before it hits the oven. That slow rise gives the crust a good crunch and makes the loaf easier on your stomach.

  • Signature Country Sourdough — $8.00 per loaf
    Our daily bread. Great for morning toast or dipping into soup.
  • Seeded Multigrain — $8.50 - $9.00 per loaf
    Loaded with sunflower seeds, flax, and toasted sesame.
  • French Baguette — $4.00 per loaf
    Crispy outside, light inside. We pull fresh batches twice a day.
  • Butter Brioche Loaf — $7.50 per loaf
    A soft, rich loaf that makes incredible French toast.

Pastries & Morning Snacks

All our pastry dough is rolled from scratch using real butter. Grab one with your morning coffee before they run out.

  • Butter Croissants — $3.75 - $4.50 each
    Flaky and layered. Available plain, filled with dark chocolate, or topped with toasted almonds.
  • Fruit Tartlets — $5.00 - $6.00 each
    Crisp buttery shell filled with vanilla cream and seasonal berries from the farmers market.
  • Cinnamon Rolls — $4.25 each
    Soft dough rolled with cinnamon sugar and finished with cream cheese frosting.
  • Daily Muffins & Scones — $3.50 - $4.00 each
    Flavors change daily. Common options are blueberry lemon, chocolate chunk, and cheddar herb.

Custom Event Cakes

We bake custom cakes for birthdays, small weddings, and family gatherings. Just give us at least 5 days notice so we can get your order on the schedule.

  • Round Layer Cakes (6-inch or 8-inch) — $35.00 - $65.00
    Popular flavors include Vanilla Bean, Dark Chocolate Fudge, and Carrot Cake.
  • Party Sheet Cakes — $50.00 - $110.00
    Feeds 20 to 50 people. Includes custom piping and simple text messages.
